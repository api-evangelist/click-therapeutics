# Click Therapeutics

> Click Therapeutics, Inc. develops, validates and commercializes software as prescription
> medical treatments — the Digital Therapeutics category it named. Founded 2012 in New York
> by David Benshoof Klein. Its AI-enabled platform treats diseases with an underlying
> neurological or behavioral component via smartphone software, used standalone or alongside
> pharmacotherapy (Software-Enhanced Drugs). Click publishes NO public API, developer portal
> or SDK: its software ships as regulated prescription therapeutics, not as a platform.
